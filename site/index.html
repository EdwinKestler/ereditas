01Locked out.

03Or triggered too early.

Dead-man switches fire on silence. A lost phone or a hospital stay should never hand out your life.

Why I built this

In 2008 my father died suddenly. To tell his clients what had happened, I had to break into his phone. To cancel his subscriptions and reach the people who mattered, I had to get into his email. His documents and photos sat behind passwords no one had. The legal succession took years; his digital life couldn't wait. Back then, automating that was impossible. Today, it isn't.

Edwin Kestler
02 The principle

Signals can ask.
Only a person can answer.

A silent phone is not proof. A memorial post is not permission. Ereditas keeps probability and authority separate: algorithms decide when to ask; a human decides whether release may begin.

01

Silence never unlocks. Evidence may ask your people—nothing more.

02

Confirmation is a gate. “Well” resets. “Unsure” escalates. Only “passed” proceeds.

03

Release stays to what you chose. The heir receives time-limited access, never a password dump.

04 Built on Meta

Meta's models read the signs.
People make the call.

Life is announced and answered inside Meta's apps. Memorial posts appear on Facebook and Instagram; the people who can confirm what happened answer on WhatsApp. Ereditas reads those signals with SAM 3.1, asks real people over WhatsApp, and uses Muse Spark to write to the heirs. Meta's apps are what make a human-confirmed digital succession feasible.
